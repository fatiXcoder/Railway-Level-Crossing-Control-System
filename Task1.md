# Task 1 — Identify Constraints

| Constraint ID | Constraint in Simple English | Why the Constraint Is Necessary |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | The barrier must not open while a train is present at the crossing. | Prevents collisions with road traffic. |
| C2 | The barrier must close when an approaching train is detected. | Stops road traffic before the train arrives. |
| C3 | Warning lights and audible alarms must activate when a train approaches. | Alerts drivers and pedestrians. |
| C4 | The barrier must remain closed until the train completely clears the crossing. | Prevents accidents during train passage. |
| C5 | The barrier must not open if sensors fail or readings are unreliable. | Prevents unsafe operation due to incorrect data. |
| C6 | The control center must be notified if the barrier fails to close. | Allows operators to take corrective action. |
| C7 | The system must maintain a safe state during communication loss. | Ensures safety despite communication failure. |
| C8 | A train must not be permitted to pass unless the barrier is confirmed closed. | Prevents trains from entering an unsafe crossing. |
| C9 | The system must activate the appropriate safety response during emergencies. | Reduces the risk of accidents. |
| C10 | Incorrect or conflicting sensor readings must not be treated as confirmation that the crossing is clear. | Prevents premature barrier opening. |
| C11 | The barrier must not open until the train is confirmed to have completely cleared the crossing. | Ensures road traffic is released safely. |
| C12 | The control center must be notified when a critical safety constraint is violated. | Supports timely intervention and fault management. |
